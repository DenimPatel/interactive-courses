<p class="section-lede" style="max-width:70ch;">Every image this guide makes starts as noise. That is not a metaphor &mdash; it is the training objective. A model is shown a picture with a little noise added, over and over, until it can name the noise in any picture, and then the naming is run backwards from a field of static until something recognisable appears. This volume builds that idea from a dataset you draw with a mouse, through the denoiser you train in the browser, up to the flow-matching transformers that produced the 2026 state of the art, and then out past images into video and sound.</p>

<p class="section-lede" style="max-width:70ch;">Nothing here assumes prior generative-model knowledge, but it does assume the foundation volumes: the <a href="{{ '/math/probability/' | relative_url }}">probability</a> guides for what a distribution is and what a conditional Gaussian looks like, and <a href="{{ '/math/probability-in-action/probabilistic-ml/' | relative_url }}">Probability in Action, Part 20</a> for the one-page diffusion on-ramp and the VAE this guide re-derives rather than assumes. The <a href="{{ '/math/linear-algebra/' | relative_url }}">linear algebra</a> guide supplies the low-rank decomposition behind the latent space and the <a href="{{ '/math/calculus-in-motion/' | relative_url }}">calculus</a> volume supplies the gradient flow that makes the denoiser trainable. Its sibling volume is <a href="{{ '/ai/multimodal/' | relative_url }}">Multimodal Models, Interactively</a>, which starts where this one leaves off: models that read images and speech as well as make them. Where this guide needs a serving number &mdash; a latency, a shard, a cost &mdash; it points at <a href="{{ '/ai/llm-serving/' | relative_url }}">LLM Serving, Interactively</a> rather than repeating it, and its 3-D cousin NeRF and Gaussian splatting live in <a href="{{ '/vision/multi-view-geometry/beyond/' | relative_url }}">multi-view geometry</a>.</p>
