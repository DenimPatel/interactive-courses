{%- comment -%} The shared footer. Two things are here that were not before. 1. A second way into the settings panel. The header control is at the top of a page that can be twenty sections long, and the reader who has just finished the last section is the one most likely to want a different text size. It is the SAME control, wired to the same dialog by id — there is no second settings surface and no second source of truth, and the dialog only ever has one `aria-expanded` to maintain, so the header button's state is the only one worth watching. 2. `focus-dim` on the footer element. That is the hook theme.css's focus mode defines: in focus mode a reader wants the prose and nothing competing with it, and a wall of links at the bottom of every page is exactly the competing thing. The hook dims the footer to 40% and restores it on hover or on `:focus-within`, so it is never a trap — a reader tabbing into the footer gets full-strength text the moment they arrive. The component styling for both the button and the footer is in assets/css/chrome.css, because theme.css is the token layer and owns no components. {%- endcomment -%}
