{%- comment -%} Single global nav for this repo's guide pages: the section hubs and the standalone interactive guide HTML files. Pages mark their subject with `section: ai|vision|math` in front matter. The field notes, the record pages and the blog live in the companion repo, denimpatel/AI, so links to those are absolute cross-repo URLs (https://denimpatel.github.io/AI/...) rather than `relative_url`, which would resolve inside this repo's baseurl. Rendered as a collapsible left-hand sidebar (fixed on desktop, an off-canvas drawer on narrow screens). Collapsing it hides the sidebar down to just its hamburger toggle, floating top-left, rather than an icon rail — the collapsed/expanded state is remembered per browser via localStorage; the mobile drawer state is not persisted. {%- endcomment -%}
