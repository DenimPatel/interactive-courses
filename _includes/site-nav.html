{%- comment -%} Global chrome for this repo's guide pages: the section hubs and the standalone interactive guide HTML files. Pages mark their subject with `section: ai|vision|math` in front matter. The field notes, the record pages and the blog live in the companion repo, denimpatel/AI, so links to those are absolute cross-repo URLs (https://denimpatel.github.io/AI/...) rather than `relative_url`, which would resolve inside this repo's baseurl. SHAPE. This is a sticky top bar: an opaque `--c-surface` plate with a hairline bottom border, the brand, the primary links, and the three reading controls on the right. It is deliberately NOT translucent. The previous design ran a liquid-glass blur through this bar, and a blur under a link label is a blur under a number the reader is about to read; an instrument panel is read, not looked through. No `backdrop-filter` appears anywhere in this chrome. Every class and id the old sidebar used is kept — `.nav`, `#site-nav`, `.nav-head`, `.nav-brand`, `.nav-brand-dot`, `.nav-collapse-toggle`, `.nav-mobile-toggle`, `.nav-mobile-toggle-bar`, `.nav-scrim` — because ~570 pages and the guide kit target them by name. What changed is the geometry, and that lives in assets/css/chrome.css at `#site-nav` specificity. The two behaviours below are unchanged in contract. Collapse still persists under `site:nav-collapsed` and still means "the primary links are out of the way", whether that is a sidebar shrinking to its toggle or a bar dropping them to a menu. The mobile drawer still opens from the hamburger, still closes on a link, on the scrim, and on Escape, and is still never persisted.
