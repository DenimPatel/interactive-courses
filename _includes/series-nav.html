{%- comment -%} Series part picker. Usage: {% include series-nav.html series=site.data.series.multi_view_geometry %} Renders the part list as a second sticky ribbon directly under the site nav (`#site-nav`), so the two bars stack: the site bar on top, this one under it, and the reader's current part stays reachable from anywhere in a part that runs to twenty sections. An earlier revision MOVED this list into `#site-nav` with a script, so that the sidebar and the part list lived in one collapsible pane. That is deliberately reversed: the site nav is now a horizontal bar, and a vertical list of parts inside it would be a menu that no control opens. The ribbon is the pane. `assets/css/chrome.css` carries the geometry, and it is sized so that the two bars together stay inside `--read-anchor-offset` (7rem) — which is what `theme.css` uses to park a `#fragment` heading clear of both. A px offset would be short by 20px at 130% text, so the whole stack is in rem. When the series declares an `acts:` list, the pills are grouped under act labels (presentational only: `num` is still the single source of part numbers, and series-prevnext.html still walks parts + appendix in flat order). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign current_part = nil -%} {%- for part in include.series.parts -%} {%- if page.url == part.permalink or page.permalink == part.permalink -%} {%- assign current_part = part -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- for extra in include.series.appendix -%} {%- if page.url == extra.permalink or page.permalink == extra.permalink -%} {%- assign current_part = extra -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
