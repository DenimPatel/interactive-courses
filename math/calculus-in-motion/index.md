<p class="section-lede" style="max-width:70ch;">Volume I built the derivative and the integral of a function. Volume II lets the function move. A vector field assigns an arrow to every point; a line integral totals those arrows along a path; divergence and curl describe how the field spreads and spins; and one theorem - Green, Stokes and the divergence theorem, all the same statement - says the total on a boundary equals the total inside it. From there it is a short step to the Jacobian determinant, to constrained optimisation, and to the matrix calculus that makes backpropagation mechanical.</p>

<p class="section-lede" style="max-width:70ch;">The last five parts are where this volume earns its keep elsewhere on the site: optimizers and the curvature that governs their convergence (handing off to <a href="{{ '/vision/nonlinear-optimization/' | relative_url }}">Nonlinear Optimization</a>), probability densities and the KL divergence, ODE integration, differentiation on a manifold of rotations, and optimising over whole trajectories. The <a href="{{ '/math/calculus/' | relative_url }}">first volume</a> is the prerequisite; nothing here needs more than it taught.</p>
