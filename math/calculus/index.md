<p class="section-lede" style="max-width:70ch;">The method is the one idea that makes the whole subject obvious: zoom in far enough on any smooth curve and it becomes a straight line. Everything else - the derivative, the chain rule, the tangent plane, the Newton step - is a consequence of that single observation, and every part lets you drag the thing being described until you can see it. No part assumes more than high-school algebra.</p>

<p class="section-lede" style="max-width:70ch;">When you finish, the gradient, Jacobian and Hessian are no longer notation you have seen: they are the derivative in one, many, and second-order form, and they connect directly to the <a href="{{ '/vision/nonlinear-optimization/' | relative_url }}">nonlinear optimization guide</a>, whose Gradient Descent, Newton and Gauss-Newton parts pick up exactly where Part 14 leaves off.</p>
